This project generates Android apps that may use AndroidX, Mozilla GeckoView, Apache Cordova, Capacitor, and OneSignal depending on selected options. Their licenses remain with their respective projects.

The UI includes small inline SVG icon paths in the style of Lucide Icons. Lucide is licensed under the ISC License.

Browser extensions are not redistributed by this repository. When GeckoView extension options are selected, the generated app requests Mozilla-signed XPI packages from the official Firefox Add-ons download endpoints at runtime. Availability and compatibility are controlled by the extension publisher and Mozilla.
